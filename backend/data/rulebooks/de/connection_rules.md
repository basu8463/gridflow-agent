# Germany — Grid Connection Rules (Demo Rulebook)

> Simplified demo rules inspired by German low-voltage connection practice
> (e.g., NAV, §14a EnWG concepts). For demonstration only.

## Rule DE-GEN-1 — Scope
All connections to the low-voltage grid require either a notification or an
approval from the responsible distribution system operator (DSO) before
commissioning.

## Rule DE-HP-1 — Heat pumps, notification procedure
Heat pumps with a rated electrical power of **12 kW or less** may use the
simplified notification procedure. The DSO must be notified at least 10
working days before commissioning.

## Rule DE-HP-2 — Heat pumps, approval procedure
Heat pumps **above 12 kW** require prior approval, including confirmation
that the device is capable of grid-friendly load management (remote power
reduction by the DSO during grid stress). Applications must include an
electrician confirmation of the load-management interface.

## Rule DE-PV-1 — Solar PV feed-in
PV systems up to **30 kW** may register via the simplified procedure.
Systems above 30 kW require a grid-compatibility study by the DSO before
approval. A site plan showing the point of connection is mandatory for all
PV applications.

## Rule DE-EV-1 — EV charging points
Charging points up to **11 kW** are notify-only. Charging points **above
11 kW** require prior approval by the DSO. The application must include the
charger datasheet and an electrician confirmation.

## Rule DE-NH-1 — New home connections
New building connections always require an application with a site plan and
a load calculation. The DSO determines the point of connection and the
connection capacity.

## Rule DE-CAP-1 — Local capacity check
Approval-track applications are checked against available local transformer
capacity. If the requested power exceeds available headroom at the local
substation, the DSO may (a) approve with load-management conditions, or
(b) require a grid reinforcement assessment.

## Rule DE-DOC-1 — Incomplete applications
If required documents are missing, the application cannot proceed and the
applicant must be asked to supply the missing documents. The processing
clock pauses until documents are complete.

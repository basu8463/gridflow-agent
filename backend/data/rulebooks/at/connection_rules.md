# Austria — Grid Connection Rules (Demo Rulebook)

> Simplified demo rules inspired by Austrian connection practice
> (TOR concepts). For demonstration only.

## Rule AT-GEN-1 — Scope
Connections to the distribution grid require a network access contract with
the responsible DSO. Notification or approval is required before
commissioning, depending on capacity.

## Rule AT-HP-1 — Heat pumps, notification procedure
Heat pumps with rated electrical power of **15 kW or less** may use the
notification procedure, provided a valid network access contract exists.

## Rule AT-HP-2 — Heat pumps, approval procedure
Heat pumps **above 15 kW** require prior approval by the DSO, including an
assessment of local grid capacity.

## Rule AT-PV-1 — Solar PV feed-in
PV systems up to **20 kW** may register via the simplified procedure.
Systems above 20 kW require DSO approval. All PV applications must include
a **TOR conformity certificate** for the inverter in addition to the
installer certificate and site plan.

## Rule AT-EV-1 — EV charging points
Charging points up to **7.4 kW** are notify-only. Charging points above
7.4 kW require prior DSO approval.

## Rule AT-NH-1 — New home connections
New building connections require an application including site plan, load
calculation, and a signed network access contract.

## Rule AT-CAP-1 — Local capacity check
Approval-track applications are assessed against local transformer
headroom. Where capacity is insufficient, the DSO may approve with
conditions or require reinforcement assessment.

## Rule AT-DOC-1 — Incomplete applications
Applications missing required documents are put on hold and the applicant
is notified of the missing items. Processing timelines restart when the
application is complete.
